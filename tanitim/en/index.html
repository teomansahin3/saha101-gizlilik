FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, login or server. Your progress stays on your phone, and you can back it up as a file to iCloud Drive from Settings.

Does it need internet?

No. All content ships inside the app, so it works on sites with no signal.

What is “tea”?

A wrong answer in a quiz costs one cup of tea. Tea brews once an hour, and passing a level for the first time gives one back. Cards stay open even with no tea. Premium has unlimited tea.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. It is iPhone only for now; Android is in the works.

Which languages?

English, Turkish, Italian, Spanish, French and German.

How far can I trust the cards?

Cards describe general site practice. Check any dimension or time against current regulations, the project and the manufacturer’s data sheet before you apply it. Mock exams are not official exams.
